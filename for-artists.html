THERE IS NO
LPX TEMPLATE.

WE START WITH THE RECORD.

Not the technology.

Not the website.

Not the effects.

We start with questions like:

What made you make this record?

What does it feel like?

What already belongs to its world?

What do you want somebody to feel when it ends?

Then we figure out what kind of place the record wants to become.

BUILD THE PLACE
YOUR RECORD DESERVES.

Build it your way.
Host it where you want.
Change it, move it, rebuild it.

Your record stays yours.

You can build an LPX yourself, use our tools, use somebody else’s tools, work with a developer, use an AI coding agent, or ask us for help.

A FEW THINGS
LPX ISN’T.

Is LPX a streaming service?

No. LPX gives your record a home. It doesn’t replace streaming.

Is LPX a website builder?

No. LPX is a format. Different tools can be used to build one.

Do I have to host with you?

No.

Is LPX an AI thing?

No. An artist can use AI or reject it completely. Neither choice is more LPX than the other.

Do I have to make something huge and interactive?

No. Sometimes the right LPX is simply music, artwork, photographs, lyrics, and notes.
